\section{Evidence policy}

Every central claim in the report must resolve to a source file, manifest, CSV, notebook output, test log, or Git checkpoint. The machine-readable registries are stored under \evidence{report/evidence}. Their purpose is to prevent accidental promotion of an old presentation value, failed experiment, or unverified administrative detail.
