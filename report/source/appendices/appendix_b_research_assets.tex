\section{Checkpoint classification}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Historical invalidated:} old DEPM DNN, ResNet, PCA, scaler, XGBoost, and classification LSTM assets.
  \item \textbf{Research archive:} PatchTST, advanced PatchTST, iTransformer, CNN-BiLSTM, and multi-horizon checkpoints available at historical Git tags.
  \item \textbf{Benchmark evidence:} ECL and Low Carbon London leaderboards and their run metadata.
  \item \textbf{Candidates:} Global N-BEATS and MultiCycleNet-adapted experimental outputs.
  \item \textbf{Packaged active checkpoints:} \breakcode{global_tft_24h}, \breakcode{global_tft_168h}, and \breakcode{month_production_v3}; frozen research, transfer, and serving-normalization metrics are reported under distinct protocols.
  \item \textbf{Packaged inactive challengers:} \breakcode{hourly_foundation_v2/release/24h} and \breakcode{168h}; both pass their frozen London gate, but deployment is deferred under the recorded resource and input-contract decision.
\end{itemize}
