2. leakage-safe feature engineering,
3. ablation studies,
4. external dataset validation,
5. baseline comparisons against proper forecasting models. ## What I would do for your PFE

For your project, I would avoid copying this paper’s binary setup unless your supervisor explicitly wants a classification task. A stronger PFE would be to:
